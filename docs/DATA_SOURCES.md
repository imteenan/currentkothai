## Known blockers

1. **DPDC uses a legacy non-Unicode Bengali font.** Feeder codes, MW loads and the hour grid
   extract cleanly, but area names come out as mojibake, so no adapter is enabled. Decoding
   the Bijoy/ASCII mapping is the single highest-value unblock after DESCO.

2. **NESCO publishes scans.** Zero extractable text; OCR on Bangla scans is not reliable
   enough to publish without human review.

3. **BREB has no national feed.** Around 80 Palli Bidyut Samity cooperatives publish
   independently, which is ~80 adapters, not one.

4. **URLs move without notice.** DPDC's schedules live on `dpdc.org.bd`, not the
   `dpdc.gov.bd` corporate site, and its zone PDFs carry an incrementing revision counter in
   the filename. Always scrape the index; never hardcode a document URL.
