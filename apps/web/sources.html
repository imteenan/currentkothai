Sources

Nothing here is our own measurement. Every number comes from a document a distributor published. Where one publishes nothing readable, we say so.

Who publishes what

DPDC zone sheets

DPDC publishes one sheet per NOCS zone. Twenty-seven are read into the schedule above: nineteen digital PDFs, plus eight scans read by detecting the ruled grid and measuring which cells are shaded. Nine scans still defeat that, so every zone links its original here.

Every registered source

Status and TLS are what the last probe observed, not what we assume. Several .gov.bd hosts serve an incomplete certificate chain, so a strict check fails even though the host is genuine, we record that rather than hide it.

What the labels mean

Official

Exactly as published, a link to their file.

Derived

Their numbers, our structure. We turned a PDF table into rows, so the original is always one click away.

Estimated

Our inference: service areas and feeder matches. Drawn dashed on the map.

Unknown

Not enough evidence. We say so instead of guessing.
